Health insurance claims fail on tiny things. We caught them.

Led a 4-person team to ship an LLM pre-checker in < 24h. Three flows — Prevention, Pre-Submission, Appeal — cover the full claim lifecycle. Shortlisted for Best Gemini API at nwHacks.

Three flows. One pipeline.

/01
Prevention flow
Pre-emptively educates users on what to gather based on procedure type and payer rules, before they touch a claim form.
/02
Pre-Submission flow
LLM validator checks submissions against 10+ required fields and flags missing documentation before submission. FastAPI + Python backend.
/03
Appeal flow
If a claim is denied, the LLM parses the denial reason and drafts an appeal letter targeting the cited grounds.
/04
Team lead · 4 people
Ideation to demo in under 24 hours. Owned scope, architecture, and the demo script.
