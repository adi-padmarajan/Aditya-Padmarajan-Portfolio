When a teen stops opening the app, you want to know before the next missed appointment.

Three years of longitudinal data. 1,500+ users, 20+ features. We surfaced four cohorts with UMAP, trained PyTorch risk flags, and shipped a dashboard four clinical teams use.

Where it moved the needle.

/01
EDA at scale
Exploratory analysis on 1,500+ longitudinal records spanning 3+ years, surfacing disengagement signals across 20+ behavioural features.
/02
UMAP cohort discovery
Reduced high-dimensional engagement data to 2D embeddings and isolated 4+ distinct user cohorts that informed downstream model design.
/03
Risk-flag pipeline
PyTorch models trained on 3+ years of longitudinal engagement data to flag at-risk youth, enabling earlier clinical intervention.
/04
Analytics dashboard
Matplotlib + GA4 + Python dashboard serving 4+ clinical and program teams — retention curves, engagement trends, risk flags.
