How do two strangers in a 5,000-person hall find the one worth talking to?

Pulse turns the venue into a radar. Three hours to build. Thirteen screens live. First of 100+ teams at Web Summit Vancouver.

What it actually does, well.

/01
AI matching engine
Claude API joined to Supabase-backed quest data, generating personalized matches and conversation openers across 12+ attendee profiles.
/02
Live spatial radar
Custom SVG animations + Framer Motion state transitions render 10+ concurrent quests across three proximity zones in real time.
/03
Thirteen synced screens
Single Supabase channel multiplexed across thirteen physical screens around the venue — every match update propagated in under 300ms.
/04
Three hour MVP
Two builders, one Figma board, no pre-written code. Shipped end-to-end in a single session and iterated live for two days.
